Who sees what

Superusers, and anyone with the “Can see every job” permission (Admin → Users), see everything. Everyone else, such as Phil, sees only jobs they hold or have ever held, plus those customers. Jobs they book in are automatically theirs; hand a job to them from its page to share it.
